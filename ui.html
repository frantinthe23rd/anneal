Runs the 5Hz language model first to plan sections, key and arrangement before the audio is generated. Off, the model improvises the whole take in one go.
The model renders at 48 kHz stereo. MP3 is capped at 128 kbps here, which is audibly lossy on cymbals and reverb tails — use FLAC unless you need small files.
Anything left blank is chosen by the model from your description.
Voice prefixes: a American, b British · f female, m male.
Schnell is distilled for 4 steps — more mostly costs time. Smaller sizes are proportionally faster.
